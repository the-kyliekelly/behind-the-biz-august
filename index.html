Here's what changed my mind about “the best” strategy

I used to think there was one. Some secret answer everyone else had already found when it came to social media. Then one day I sat down and looked at how many people my Instagram posts were actually sending to my website. Barely any. All that daily effort, and nothing to show for it.

That was when I stopped putting all my eggs in the social media basket, and started asking myself: how could I do this differently? Which strategy fits this season of my business, as a mum to two little kids?

A strategy that works at 500 subscribers isn't the one that works at 5,000. A strategy that works with four hours a week isn't the one that works with forty.

So instead of handing you my one opinion, I sat down with seven women who are each brilliant at something different and got them talking. This week, you get all of it laid out, so you can see the whole map, and pick the strategy that fits this season of your business instead of continuing to wing it.
